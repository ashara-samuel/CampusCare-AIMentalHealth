CampusCare — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

CampusCare is a supportive wellbeing tool for students — not a therapist, doctor, diagnosis, or crisis service. It does not create a doctor–patient relationship. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line.

This policy explains what CampusCare ("we", "the app") collects, why, and the choices you have. It covers the web app and the Android and iOS apps.

Who we are

CampusCare is operated by [Your name / organisation]. For any privacy question, contact [your contact email].

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, an optional display name, and your password (stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash; we never store your actual password).
  • Your notes — any private notes you write in the app.
  • Your conversations — the messages you send to the wellbeing companion and its replies, so the conversation has continuity. This can include sensitive information you choose to share about how you feel.
  • Basic technical data — standard server and security logs (e.g. request times). We do not log the content of your messages, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not ask for more than the above.

How we use it

  • To provide the service — sign you in, store your notes, and let the companion respond with awareness of your recent conversation.
  • To keep the service safe — a safety layer checks messages for signs of crisis so the app can show you real support resources.
  • To secure and maintain the service.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

When a message is processed by an AI provider

To generate a reply, the text of your message may be sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) solely to produce the response and to run the safety check. These providers process the text under their own terms and do not receive your account identity from us. Voice playback, where enabled, is produced on your own device and sends nothing to us or a third party. If you prefer not to have messages processed this way, do not use the chat feature.

How your data is protected

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — never stored in readable form.
  • Your notes and conversations are isolated to your account; no other user can read them.
  • Access requires an authenticated session (token-based).
  • Traffic is served over HTTPS.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, notes, and conversation history until you delete them. You can clear your conversation history at any time, and you can delete your whole account (see below).

Your choices and rights

  • Clear your conversation — the "Clear" control removes your stored conversation history.
  • Delete your account — the "Delete account" control (or DELETE /auth/me) permanently deletes your account and all associated data (notes and conversations). This cannot be undone.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (to access, correct, export, or restrict processing of your data). Contact us to exercise them.

Crisis situations

If the app detects possible self-harm or crisis signals in a message, it responds by showing you crisis resources and encouraging you to reach a real person. The app does not automatically contact emergency services or share your information with third parties on your behalf.

Children and young people

CampusCare is intended for students and is not directed to children under 13. If you are under the age of majority where you live, please use CampusCare with the awareness of a parent, guardian, or trusted adult. If you believe a child has provided us personal data without appropriate consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Where your data is processed

The service is hosted on cloud infrastructure (Render, in the United States). If you use CampusCare from another country, your data is processed in the location of that infrastructure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; we will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, provide notice in the app.
